# AudioX / Audio-Omni

Audio-Omni is the first end-to-end framework unifying understanding, generation, and editing across general sound, music, and speech domains. Presented at SIGGRAPH 2026. AudioX is a unified framework integrating text, video, image, and audio conditions.

## Innovation

First unified framework covering all three audio domains. Combines frozen multimodal LLM (Qwen2.5-Omni) with trainable Diffusion Transformer for high-fidelity synthesis. Any-to-any audio processing.
