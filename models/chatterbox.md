# Chatterbox

Family of SOTA open-source TTS models by Resemble AI, covering a single-language English line plus a multilingual V3 release that brings broader language coverage, more consistent speaker similarity, reduced hallucinations, and more natural conversational speech across 23+ languages.

## Links

- HuggingFace: https://huggingface.co/ResembleAI/chatterbox
- GitHub: https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox
- Website: https://resemble.ai/
- Demo: https://huggingface.co/spaces/ResembleAI/Chatterbox-Multilingual-TTS-V3

## Features

- parameters: 500M (Llama backbone, 0.5B)
- voice_cloning: yes (zero-shot, multi-language)
- asr: no
- pronunciation: yes
- emotion_control: yes (exaggeration / intensity control)
- languages: 23+
- streaming: no
- license: MIT

## Comparison

- voice_cloning: ✅
- asr: ❌
- languages: 23+
- streaming: ❌
- license: MIT

## Innovation

First open-source TTS model with explicit **emotion exaggeration control**, plus an alignment-informed inference pipeline and a watermarked decoder. Multilingual V3 narrows the quality gap to closed systems like ElevenLabs on cross-language voice cloning while staying under 1B parameters.
