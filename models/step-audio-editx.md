# Step-Audio-EditX

3B-parameter LLM-based RL audio model specialized in expressive and iterative audio editing.

## Links

- HuggingFace: https://huggingface.co/stepfun-ai/Step-Audio-EditX
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.03601

## Features

- parameters: 3B (4B BF16)
- voice_cloning: yes
- asr: no
- pronunciation: yes (Polyphone)
- emotion_control: yes
- languages: Mandarin, English, Sichuanese, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean
- streaming: yes
- license: Apache-2.0
