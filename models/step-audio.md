# Step-Audio

Production-ready open-source framework for intelligent speech interaction with unified speech comprehension and generation.

## Links

- GitHub: https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step-Audio

- parameters: 130B (Chat), 3B (TTS)
- voice_cloning: yes

- pronunciation: yes

- languages: Chinese, English, Japanese
- streaming: yes
