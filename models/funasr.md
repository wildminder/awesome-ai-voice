# FunASR

Industrial-grade end-to-end speech recognition toolkit with SOTA pretrained models, supporting ASR, VAD, punctuation, language modeling, speaker verification, speaker diarization, and multi-talker ASR across 50+ languages. Architectures are updated frequently and the project tracks upstream ModelScope releases.
