# Voxtral-4B-TTS

Frontier, open-weights text-to-speech model developed by Mistral AI. Designed to be fast, instantly adaptable, and produces lifelike speech with natural prosody and emotional range.

## Links

- HuggingFace: https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Voxtral-4B-TTS-2603
- Demo: https://console.mistral.ai/build/audio/text-to-speech
- Blog: https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-tts

## Features

- parameters: 4B
- voice_cloning: yes
- asr: no
- pronunciation: no
- emotion_control: yes
- languages: 9 (En, Fr, Es, De, It, Pt, Nl, Ar, Hi)
- streaming: yes (RTF 0.103 at concurrency 1)
- audio_output: 24 kHz
