# Qwen3-TTS

Qwen3-TTS is an open-source series of Text-to-Speech models developed by Alibaba Cloud. Supports stable, expressive, and streaming speech generation with free-form voice design.

## Links

- GitHub: https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS
- HuggingFace: https://huggingface.co/collections/Qwen/qwen3-tts
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15621

## Features

- parameters: 0.6B-1.7B
- voice_cloning: yes (3-second)
- asr: no
- pronunciation: yes
- emotion_control: yes
- languages: 10 (Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Russian, Portuguese, Spanish, Italian)
- streaming: yes (97ms latency)
- license: Apache-2.0
