# SoulX-Singer

SoulX-Singer is a high-fidelity, zero-shot singing voice synthesis model for generating realistic singing voices for unseen singers without fine-tuning.

## Links

- HuggingFace: https://huggingface.co/spaces/Soul-AILab/SoulX-Singer
